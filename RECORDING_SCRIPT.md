## 🎥 RECORDING TIPS

### **Before Recording**
1. **Close unnecessary apps** - Clean desktop, no notifications
2. **Test audio** - Record 10 seconds, play back to check quality
3. **Set terminal size** - Large enough to read, but fits screen nicely
4. **Clear terminal** - `clear` command before starting
5. **Have water nearby** - Stay hydrated for clear voice

### **During Recording**
1. **Speak clearly** - Not too fast, not too slow
2. **Show enthusiasm** - This is exciting technology!
3. **Pause naturally** - Let the script's delays work, don't rush
4. **Point with cursor** - When showing HashScan links or important info
5. **If you make a mistake** - Pause, take a breath, restart that section

### **Key Phrases to Use**
- "Exclusively on Hedera" (say 2-3 times)
- "Can't be done on any other blockchain" (say 2 times)
- "All 4 Hedera services" (mention in opening and closing)
- "Verifiable on HashScan" (whenever showing links)
- "Production-ready" (in summary)
